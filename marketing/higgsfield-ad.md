# Tadbirly — "ONE TAP" ad (Higgsfield)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, ~24 s, for Instagram Reels / TikTok / Telegram.
**Idea:** A bored student in Tashkent gets one Tadbirly notification. They tap it, and
the phone *throws them* through every opportunity in Uzbekistan — hackathon, MUN,
internship, team, stage — each jump a crash zoom through the phone screen. It ends
back on the same bench, but now they're a different person.

**Tagline:** *Every opportunity in Uzbekistan — one tap away.*
**Uzbek:** *Barcha imkoniyatlar — bir bosishda.*

---

## Before you start (free plan)

- Free credits are limited, so **generate 1 take per shot** first and only re-roll the
  ones that look bad. Shots 1, 3 and 8 matter most.
- **Keep the same hero.** Generate the hero image first (shot 0), then use it as the
  start frame / reference image for every video shot ("image to video").
- **Don't make the AI draw the app.** AI-generated phone screens come out garbled. Film the
  phone screen as plain green/blank, then put real Tadbirly screenshots on top in CapCut.
- Keep every video shot at the **shortest length** (≈3–5 s); you'll cut them tighter anyway.

## Music & sound

- Quiet lo-fi for shots 1–2, then a **hard bass drop exactly on the TAP** (shot 3).
- Fast trap/phonk beat for 4–8, every cut on a beat.
- Whoosh on each transition, record-scratch/stop on the bullet-time freeze, beat
  cuts out for the calm final shot, logo "ding".
- Use CapCut's built-in licensed music (or TikTok/Instagram's library) to avoid
  copyright strikes.

## Editing in CapCut (free)

1. Drop shots 1–9 on the timeline, trim each to the length in the table.
2. Shot 2: place a real screenshot of a Tadbirly notification over the phone screen
   (Overlay → Mask → rectangle, track it to the phone).
3. Add the on-screen words in a heavy bold font, white with the brand green
   `#2d5a3d` outline, popping in on the beat.
4. Speed-ramp shots 4, 6 and 8 (fast → slow → fast).
5. Add the end card, export 1080×1920.
